## Common Beans (Phaseolus vulgaris)

### Black Beans
- **Appearance**: Small, shiny, black exterior with creamy white interior
- **Flavor**: Rich, earthy, slightly sweet
- **Uses**: Cuban black beans and rice, black bean soup, Mexican cuisine, salads
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (soaked)

### Navy Beans (White Beans)
- **Appearance**: Small, oval, creamy white
- **Flavor**: Mild, creamy texture
- **Uses**: Boston baked beans, white bean soup, cassoulet
- **Cooking Time**: 45-60 minutes (soaked)

### Kidney Beans
- **Appearance**: Large, kidney-shaped, deep red
- **Flavor**: Full-bodied, slightly sweet
- **Uses**: Chili con carne, red beans and rice, three-bean salad
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (soaked)

### Pinto Beans
- **Appearance**: Medium size, beige with brown speckles (become pink when cooked)
- **Flavor**: Earthy, nutty
- **Uses**: Refried beans, Mexican cuisine, chili
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (soaked)

### Cannellini Beans
- **Appearance**: Large, white, kidney-shaped
- **Flavor**: Mild, nutty, creamy texture
- **Uses**: Italian cuisine, minestrone, white bean salads
- **Cooking Time**: 45-60 minutes (soaked)

### Great Northern Beans
- **Appearance**: Medium-large, white, oval
- **Flavor**: Mild, slightly nutty
- **Uses**: Baked beans, soups, stews
- **Cooking Time**: 45-75 minutes (soaked)

### Romano Beans (Borlotti)
- **Appearance**: Large, creamy with red/brown speckles
- **Flavor**: Sweet, nutty, creamy
- **Uses**: Italian cuisine, pasta e fagioli, salads
- **Cooking Time**: 45-60 minutes (soaked)

## Lima Beans (Phaseolus lunatus)

### Baby Lima Beans
- **Appearance**: Small, flat, pale green or cream
- **Flavor**: Delicate, buttery
- **Uses**: Succotash, side dishes, soups
- **Cooking Time**: 45-60 minutes (soaked)

### Large Lima Beans (Fordhook)
- **Appearance**: Large, flat, cream colored
- **Flavor**: Rich, buttery, meaty texture
- **Uses**: Side dishes, casseroles, stews
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (soaked)

---

## Runner Beans (Phaseolus coccineus)

### Scarlet Runner Beans
- **Appearance**: Large, flat, purple and black mottled
- **Flavor**: Rich, meaty, slightly sweet
- **Uses**: Traditional European dishes, stews, as fresh green beans
- **Cooking Time**: 90-120 minutes (soaked)

---

## Soybeans (Glycine max)

### Edamame
- **Appearance**: Fresh, green, fuzzy pods
- **Flavor**: Sweet, nutty, grassy
- **Uses**: Steamed as snack, salads, Asian cuisine
- **Cooking Time**: 5-10 minutes (fresh)

### Black Soybeans
- **Appearance**: Small, black, round
- **Flavor**: Rich, complex, slightly sweet
- **Uses**: Asian cuisine, fermented products, soups
- **Cooking Time**: 90-120 minutes (soaked)

---

## Black-eyed Peas (Vigna unguiculata)

### Black-eyed Peas
- **Appearance**: Cream colored with distinctive black "eye"
- **Flavor**: Earthy, slightly nutty
- **Uses**: Southern cuisine, Hoppin' John, salads, fritters
- **Cooking Time**: 45-60 minutes (soaked)

---

## Chickpeas (Cicer arietinum)

### Garbanzo Beans (Kabuli)
- **Appearance**: Large, round, beige/tan
- **Flavor**: Nutty, earthy, slightly grainy
- **Uses**: Hummus, Mediterranean cuisine, salads, curries
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (soaked)

### Desi Chickpeas
- **Appearance**: Small, dark, angular
- **Flavor**: More intense, nuttier than kabuli
- **Uses**: Indian cuisine, ground into flour (besan)
- **Cooking Time**: 45-75 minutes (soaked)

---

## Lentils (Lens culinaris)

### Green Lentils (French/Le Puy)
- **Appearance**: Small, dark green/gray, speckled
- **Flavor**: Peppery, earthy, firm texture
- **Uses**: Salads, side dishes, French cuisine
- **Cooking Time**: 20-25 minutes (no soaking needed)

### Red Lentils
- **Appearance**: Small, orange-red (turn yellow when cooked)
- **Flavor**: Mild, sweet, quick-cooking
- **Uses**: Indian dal, soups, Middle Eastern cuisine
- **Cooking Time**: 15-20 minutes (no soaking needed)

### Brown Lentils
- **Appearance**: Small, brown, round
- **Flavor**: Mild, earthy
- **Uses**: Soups, stews, lentil loaf
- **Cooking Time**: 25-30 minutes (no soaking needed)

### Black Beluga Lentils
- **Appearance**: Very small, black, shiny (like caviar)
- **Flavor**: Rich, earthy, firm texture
- **Uses**: Salads, side dishes, gourmet presentations
- **Cooking Time**: 20-25 minutes (no soaking needed)

---

## Fava Beans (Vicia faba)

### Broad Beans
- **Appearance**: Large, flat, light green (fresh) or brown (dried)
- **Flavor**: Rich, slightly bitter, creamy
- **Uses**: Mediterranean cuisine, purees, side dishes
- **Cooking Time**: 60-90 minutes (dried, soaked)

## Cooking Tips

### General Guidelines
- **Soaking**: Most dried beans benefit from 6-8 hours soaking (except lentils)
- **Salt**: Add salt only after beans are tender to prevent tough skins
- **Aromatics**: Bay leaves, onions, and garlic enhance flavor during cooking
- **Storage**: Cooked beans keep 3-5 days refrigerated, 6 months frozen

### Preparation Methods
1. **Stovetop**: Traditional method, good control over texture
2. **Pressure Cooker**: Reduces cooking time by 60-70%
3. **Slow Cooker**: Convenient for hands-off cooking
4. **Oven**: Good for baked bean dishes

All beans are excellent sources of:
- **Protein**: Essential amino acids (combine with grains for complete protein)
- **Fiber**: Both soluble and insoluble for digestive health
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: Steady energy release
- **Minerals**: Iron, potassium, magnesium, folate
- **Antioxidants**: Various phytonutrients for health benefits
