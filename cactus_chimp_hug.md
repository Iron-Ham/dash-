# Cactus Hugging Apprehensive Chimpanzee

Here's a cool ASCII art drawing of a cactus giving a hug to a very apprehensive chimpanzee:

```
                    .-""""""-.
                   /          \
                  |  O      O  |  <-- Very apprehensive chimp
                  |      ^     |      
                  |    \___/   |  "This seems... prickly..."
                   \   ~~~~   /
                    '-......-'
                      |    |
```

*The cactus's spines are drawn as asterisks (*) and the chimp's worried expression shows just how apprehensive they are about this prickly embrace!*

## Description
This ASCII art depicts:
- A worried chimpanzee with big round eyes and a frown
- A friendly cactus with spines (asterisks) extending its arms 
- The cactus reassuring the chimp with a speech bubble
- The chimp's thought bubble showing concern about the prickly situation

The scene captures the humor of an unlikely friendship between two very different characters!
